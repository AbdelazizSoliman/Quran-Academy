1. Readiness assessment

Is the application 100% complete? No software can honestly be certified as covering 100% of every academy's needs without production acceptance testing. The application is a broad Quran Academy MVP with the primary academic and operational workflows implemented. Production readiness still depends on real-user testing, data review, monitoring, backups, provider configuration, and policy approval.

Implemented scope

  • Secure administrator-created accounts and single-use invitations.
  • Teacher, student, staff, guardian, program, offering, and enrollment management.
  • Teacher availability, lesson scheduling, conflict checks, and role schedules.
  • Lesson check-in, start, attendance, completion, locking, and audit history.
  • Academic lesson reports, individual student entries, and manual communications.
  • Assessments, exams, progress summaries, transcripts, and certificate records.
  • Teacher payroll preparation, approval, payment recording, dashboards, reports, and CSV exports.
  • Email/WhatsApp notification records, delivery attempts, failures, and retry workflows.
  • Arabic RTL and English LTR interfaces with role-based authorization.

Important limitations

  • Certificate records do not generate formal certificate PDFs.
  • Payroll is operational tracking, not accounting, banking, taxation, or government payroll.
  • WhatsApp delivery requires approved Meta templates, valid E.164 numbers, and provider configuration.
  • Email delivery requires a configured Resend account and verified sender domain.
  • Reminder jobs require an external scheduler in production.
  • Guardians do not have authenticated portal accounts.
  • Before launch, perform browser, mobile, accessibility, backup/restore, load, and security acceptance testing.

2. Getting started

Step 1 — Open the application. Visit the application URL and select Arabic or English where the interface offers a locale control.
Step 2 — Sign in. Open /account/sign-in, enter the email and password, then choose Sign in.
Step 3 — Use the sidebar. Available items vary by role. If a page is not visible, the signed-in role is not authorized for that operation.
Step 4 — Confirm the time zone. Scheduled and actual times should be reviewed in the user's configured time zone and academy time-zone context.
Step 5 — Sign out safely. Use the account menu and Sign out, especially on shared devices.

3. Administrator guide

3.1 Initial academy setup

  1. Open Settings from the administration sidebar.
  2. Review academy identity, time zone, lateness rules, teacher check-in windows, payroll defaults, grade boundaries, and notification settings.
  3. Choose invitation delivery mode: Email Only, WhatsApp Only, or Email + WhatsApp.
  4. Enable only notification channels whose production credentials and sender/template approvals are complete.
  5. Save, reopen Settings, and confirm values persisted.

3.2 Create users and send invitations

  1. Open Users and choose New User.
  2. Enter name, email, role, required phone number, and optional WhatsApp number. If WhatsApp is blank, phone is used.
  3. Submit. The user and role profile are created together; the account remains pending.
  4. The invitation service creates one secure, expiring, single-use token and dispatches according to academy settings.
  5. Open Invitations to inspect status. Use Resend to invalidate the old token and issue a new one, or Cancel to block acceptance.
  6. For a failed WhatsApp/email attempt, open Notification Center, inspect provider details, correct configuration or recipient data, then use Retry.
Security: never send a default password. Administrators should not ask users to share their password or invitation token.

3.3 Configure teachers and students

  1. Open Teachers; complete the teacher profile, status, qualifications, contact data, salary strategy, and rate where applicable.
  2. Open Students; complete student type, time zone, contact preferences, and academic profile.
  3. For a minor, create or select a guardian and create a guardianship. Mark the correct primary contact.
  4. Keep operational records active/inactive or archived through lifecycle controls; avoid deleting historical data.

3.4 Programs, offerings, and enrollment

  1. Create a reusable Program, enter Arabic/English names and defaults, then activate it.
  2. Create a Course Offering from the program, set capacity and dates, then move it through Open and In Progress when appropriate.
  3. Create an Enrollment linking a student to an offering.
  4. Review placement and guardian readiness where required.
  5. Use explicit actions to approve, waitlist, activate, pause, complete, cancel, withdraw, transfer, or archive.

4. Administrator scheduling and attendance

4.1 Teacher availability

  1. Open Teacher Availability and create weekly availability windows.
  2. Add date-specific exceptions for leave, closures, or special availability.
  3. Review local time and time zone carefully, especially around daylight-saving changes.

4.2 Schedule a lesson

  1. Open Scheduled Lessons and choose New.
  2. Select offering, assigned teacher, start/end time, lesson title, and meeting information.
  3. Add eligible participants. The application checks availability and scheduling conflicts.
  4. Schedule the draft. Use the explicit Reschedule or Cancel action for later changes.
  5. Do not edit scheduled timestamps to represent actual operation; actual start/end fields are recorded separately.

4.3 Operate and close a lesson

  1. Open the lesson and use administrator override check-in only when necessary; record the reason.
  2. Start the lesson. This records actual start time and opens attendance.
  3. For each expected participant, record arrival/present, absent, excused absence, or departure.
  4. Review unresolved students before completion. Confirm explicitly if unresolved students must become absent.
  5. Complete the lesson. Actual end time is recorded and attendance is locked.
  6. If correction is required, reopen attendance as an administrator with a reason, adjust it, and relock.

4.4 Attendance review

  1. Open Attendance to filter by date, teacher, program, offering, lesson status, student, or attendance status.
  2. Use late, absent, and unresolved filters for operational follow-up.
  3. Open a lesson to review teacher check-in, actual lesson times, attendance lock state, participant outcomes, and audit history.

5. Administrator academic workflows

5.1 Lesson reports

  1. After a completed lesson has locked attendance, open Lesson Reports.
  2. Review the teacher's summary, topics, homework, next lesson plan, and per-student entries.
  3. Confirm private teacher notes are not copied into student or guardian communications.
  4. Use Review, then Lock to finalize. Use Reopen with a required reason for corrections, followed by Relock.

5.2 Assessments and progress

  1. Create and activate Assessment Categories.
  2. Create an Assessment Template and add ordered numeric or rating rubric items with weights.
  3. Create a student assessment against an enrollment, assigned teacher, date, and optional lesson.
  4. Enter rubric results and notes. Use lifecycle actions: Draft → Submitted → Reviewed → Published → Archived.
  5. Only published assessments should be considered student-facing.
  6. Open Student Progress to review averages, highest/lowest scores, category strengths/weaknesses, Quran position, completion, readiness, and trend.

5.3 Exams and certificates

  1. Create an Exam Session for a program/offering and teacher.
  2. Move it through Draft, Scheduled, Completed, Reviewed, and Published using explicit actions.
  3. Create a Certificate for program completion, exam completion, or Ijazah.
  4. Verify student, issue date, issuer, type, and verification code before notifying the recipient.
  5. Remember that certificate PDF generation is not included; the application stores and displays the certificate record.

5.4 Academic dashboard

Use the Academic Dashboard to review assessment/exam statistics, pass rate, high performers, and students needing attention. Treat these indicators as decision support, not as a substitute for teacher review.

6. Administrator payroll, reporting, and notifications

6.1 Teacher payroll

  1. Confirm teacher salary strategy and rate are correct before generating payroll.
  2. Open Teacher Payrolls, choose New, select teacher and period, then generate the draft.
  3. Review completed lessons included; cancelled and draft lessons must be excluded.
  4. Review base amount, bonus, deduction, manual adjustment, and net formula.
  5. Use Prepare, then Approve, then Mark Paid. These actions record operational state only; they do not transfer money.
  6. If correction is needed before final payment recording, use Reopen where allowed and retain the audit trail.
Net amount: Base + Bonus − Deduction + Manual Adjustment.

6.2 Operational reports and CSV

  1. Open Operational Reports.
  2. Select teacher workload, teacher attendance, student attendance, lesson completion, lesson reports, payroll, programs, offerings, or enrollments.
  3. Apply a finite date range and relevant teacher/program/course/status filters.
  4. Review totals, then choose CSV Export. The export is UTF-8 BOM encoded for Arabic-compatible spreadsheet import.

6.3 Notification Center

  1. Open Notification Center to view recipient, channel, provider, status, attempt count, timestamps, provider message ID, and response.
  2. Open an item for its timeline, audit events, and immutable delivery attempts.
  3. Use manual actions to send eligible invitation, lesson reminder, lesson report, or certificate notifications.
  4. Retry only failed notifications after correcting the underlying phone, email, template, credential, or provider issue.
  5. A successful API acceptance does not necessarily prove handset delivery; inspect provider status and Meta/Resend dashboards when needed.

7. Staff guide

Staff access is intentionally read-only for sensitive operational areas. The visible interface may resemble administration, but mutation actions are restricted by authorization.
  1. Sign in with the staff account and use the sidebar to open permitted dashboards and lists.
  2. Review teachers, students, programs, offerings, enrollments, schedules, attendance, lesson reports, and operational reports where exposed.
  3. Use filters and pagination rather than exporting or scanning unrestricted data unnecessarily.
  4. Review notification history only where staff policy permits it; do not expect retry, reopen, lifecycle, payroll mutation, or user-administration controls.
  5. Escalate corrections to an active administrator instead of attempting workarounds.
  6. Never share student, guardian, payroll, invitation, or notification information outside approved academy operations.

Staff verification checklist

  • Mutation buttons are absent or denied.
  • Private teacher notes and sensitive audit payloads are not exposed.
  • Reports show only the intended academy data.
  • No payroll approval/payment or invitation management action is available.

8. Teacher guide

8.1 Profile and availability

  1. Open Profile and verify name, phone/WhatsApp, locale, and time zone.
  2. Open Availability and maintain recurring weekly windows.
  3. Add availability exceptions for specific unavailable or specially available dates.

8.2 Conduct a lesson

  1. Open Schedule. Only lessons assigned to the signed-in teacher should appear.
  2. Open the lesson and choose Check In within the configured check-in window.
  3. Choose Start Lesson. Attendance opens and actual start time is recorded.
  4. For each student, record live arrival or the appropriate attendance outcome. Lateness is calculated from scheduled start, not actual start.
  5. Record departure for present/late students where needed.
  6. Resolve every expected participant. On completion, review the unresolved warning and explicitly confirm absent defaults if appropriate.
  7. Choose Complete Lesson. Attendance locks; teachers cannot reopen it.

8.3 Complete the lesson report

  1. From the completed lesson, open or initialize its report.
  2. Enter a meaningful lesson summary, topics, general homework, and next lesson plan.
  3. Open each expected student's entry and record reading, memorization, revision, Tajweed, engagement, performance, homework, and next target.
  4. Keep private notes private; use student-visible and guardian-visible fields deliberately.
  5. Complete all required entries and Submit. A submitted report becomes non-editable to the teacher unless an administrator reopens it.

8.4 Assessments, exams, notifications, and payroll

  1. Open Assessments to create or edit only assessments assigned to you, then submit them for review.
  2. Open Exams to view assigned sessions and participants; administrative lifecycle controls remain unavailable.
  3. Open Notifications to review your notification history and permitted manual communication records.
  4. Open Payroll to view only your own periods, calculations, lesson details, and status. Teachers cannot prepare, approve, reopen, cancel, or mark payroll paid.
  5. Use the teacher dashboard/reports for your own workload and student follow-up only.

9. Student guide

9.1 Account activation

  1. Open the secure invitation link received by email or WhatsApp.
  2. Check the displayed academy and account information; the invitation does not allow email or role editing.
  3. Enter and confirm a strong password, then activate the account.
  4. The invitation becomes unusable after acceptance or expiration. Contact the academy if it has expired.
  5. Sign in normally after activation.

9.2 Daily use

  1. Open Profile to review or update permitted personal preferences.
  2. Open Enrollments to see your programs, offerings, enrollment status, and placement information.
  3. Open Schedule for your linked lessons. Meeting details follow lesson lifecycle and visibility rules.
  4. Open Attendance to see only your own outcome, arrival, lateness, and excuse state.
  5. Open Lesson Reports to see reviewed/locked reports and your own completed, non-withheld entry.
  6. Open Assessments for your published grades and feedback.
  7. Open Progress for academic averages, category indicators, Quran progress, and trend.
  8. Open Transcript for programs, enrollments, assessments, grades, certificates, and overall progress.
  9. Open Certificates to view issued certificate records and verification codes.
  10. Open Notifications for your own notification history.
Students cannot edit attendance, reports, assessments, certificates, notifications, or another student's information. Draft, unreviewed, private, withheld, and administrative content is intentionally hidden.

11. Production launch checklist

  • Verify Render environment variables without printing secrets.
  • Verify database backups and perform a restore rehearsal.
  • Test invitation acceptance, expiry, resend, and cancellation.
  • Test approved Meta templates in both configured languages and validate real delivery.
  • Test Resend sender verification, bounce/failure behavior, and links using HTTPS.
  • Configure an external scheduler for reminder sweep jobs if reminders are required.
  • Run full automated verification and resolve every failure/security warning.
  • Perform role-isolation tests for admin, staff, teacher, and multiple students.
  • Verify Arabic RTL, English LTR, keyboard operation, and 390px mobile layout.
  • Confirm academy policies for attendance corrections, report publication, payroll approval, retention, and privacy.
  • Train administrators with non-production data before entering real student information.
